THE BLUEPRINT

Our design, fabrication, and track development workflow.

01/

CAD Structural Simulation

Chassis components are modeled in SolidWorks and subjected to strict Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to maximize torsional stiffness while minimizing raw mass.

02/

TIG Welding & Custom Fab

We hand-weld our chromoly steel frames within Lab 4, refining steering knuckles and weight distributions to target optimal 40/60 balancing.

03/

Data Acquisition & Telemetry

Real-time lap tracking, sensor-logged tire temperatures, and engine rpm curves help drivers extract peak performance on track.
